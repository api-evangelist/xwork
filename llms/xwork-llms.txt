# XWORK

> XWORK (xwork.co) is an Indonesian marketplace for booking workspace on demand: meeting rooms, event spaces, private offices, coworking desks, co-living, and virtual offices (including company-incorporation "Pendirian PT" packages) across Indonesian cities such as Jakarta and Surabaya. Bookings are made through the website with instant-book and payment options; XWORK is backed by 500 Global.

XWORK publishes no public developer program: no developer portal, API documentation,
OpenAPI specification, or /.well-known/ discovery files were found on xwork.co
(probed 2026-07-21). This file was generated by API Evangelist from the company's
public website for the api-evangelist/xwork profile.

## Product

- [Meeting rooms](https://xwork.co/id/list-area-all/ruang-meeting): Book meeting rooms by the hour across Indonesia
- [Office space](https://xwork.co/id/list-area-all/ruang-kantor): Serviced and private offices
- [Coworking](https://xwork.co/id/coworking-space): Coworking desks and hot desks
- [Meeting packages](https://xwork.co/id/list-area-all/paket-meeting): Bundled meeting packages
- [List your space](https://xwork.co/register-room): Register a room as a space partner
